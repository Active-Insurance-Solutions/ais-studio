Active Insurance Solutions does not use cookies on this website to track, identify, or profile visitors. We've intentionally built this site to be privacy-first, and we believe you shouldn't have to trade your personal data for the ability to read about insurance.

## What This Means

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device so it can remember things about you between page loads or visits. Common uses include keeping you logged in, remembering items in a shopping cart, and tracking your browsing for advertising. **Our website does none of these things.** You'll never see a cookie consent banner here because there's nothing for you to consent to.

## Analytics

We use Simple Analytics to understand which pages on our site are popular, what referrers send us traffic, and which devices our visitors use. Simple Analytics is fundamentally different from most analytics platforms:

- **No cookies of any kind** are set on your device
- **No personal data** is collected or stored
- **No cross-site tracking** — Simple Analytics cannot follow you to other websites
- **No fingerprinting** — your device, IP, and identity are not used to build a profile

Simple Analytics is GDPR-, CCPA-, and PECR-compliant by design and is widely used by privacy-conscious businesses. You can learn more at [simpleanalytics.com/privacy](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/privacy).

## Third-Party Embeds

Our Contact page includes a Google Maps embed so visitors can see our office location. **Google may set its own cookies** when this map loads. We do not control those cookies, do not receive any data from them, and do not use them for any purpose. If you'd prefer to avoid them, you can:

- Skip the Contact page and reach us by phone or email instead
- Use a browser extension or setting that blocks third-party cookies
- Use a privacy-focused browser such as Brave, Firefox with Enhanced Tracking Protection, or Safari with Cross-Site Tracking Prevention enabled

For details on cookies set by Google services, see Google's own privacy notice at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Local Storage and Session Storage

The site may briefly use your browser's built-in local storage to remember small interface preferences (for example, whether you've chosen light or dark mode). This data:

- Lives entirely on your device — it is never transmitted to us or anyone else
- Is not a cookie and cannot be used to track you
- Can be cleared at any time through your browser's storage settings

## Server Logs

Like any website on the public internet, our hosting provider keeps short-term server logs that include the IP address of devices that connect to our servers. These logs are used only to diagnose technical problems and protect the site from abuse, and they are not used to identify individual visitors or build a profile of anyone's browsing behavior.
