Active Insurance Solutions is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive experience so that everyone — regardless of ability — can access the information and services they need.

## Our Commitment

We aim to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines help make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

## Measures We Take

To support accessibility, our website includes:

- Semantic HTML markup that works with assistive technologies such as screen readers
- Sufficient color contrast between text and background
- Keyboard-navigable interactive elements, including menus, accordions, and forms
- Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images
- Clear focus indicators on links, buttons, and form fields
- Form labels associated with their inputs
- Skip-to-content links for screen reader and keyboard users

## Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our website against current accessibility standards and make improvements as needed. As we update the site or add new features, we work to keep accessibility a priority.

## Third-Party Content

Some portions of our website may rely on third-party content or integrations (for example, embedded maps or analytics scripts). While we do our best to choose accessible third-party providers, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of content we do not control.
